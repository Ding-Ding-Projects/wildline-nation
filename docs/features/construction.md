# Template homes and construction crews

Northline Builders is a physical place. At its counter, the player selects the Harbour Courtyard 01 template, sees its footprint and accessibility summary, hires Jo, Ren, and Akiko, and signs the construction contract. The current slice uses a compressed ten-phase sequence: survey, foundation, frame, roof, windows, utility rough-in, insulation, interior finish, exterior finish, and inspection/handover.

The builder panel is contextual to the building office. The map, pause controls, inventory, and remote progress information cannot buy a house or hire workers. The full Yum Tong implementation will add generated interior validation, materials, weather, inspections, worker schedules, renovation, furnishing, and SQLite/WAL persistence.
