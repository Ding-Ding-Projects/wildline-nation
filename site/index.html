Physical terminals

Buy a route-specific one-way ticket inside the correct ferry terminal or airport, then board from that same building.

▣

Ride every city

Bus, subway, and five-stop streetcar loops are covered by the basic national pass in Harbourlight, Gullhaven, and Asterfield.

◈

Unique local life

Each city has its own creature, arena, minigame, builder, services, palette, and progress rather than copied placeholders.

LIFE, NOT A CHECKLIST

Small errands become a life.

Eat at Copper Kettle, stock up at Moss & Market, book a haircut, take a shift, make a friend, or play the city’s ten-room Minigame Centre. Battles are a paying profession, not a toll booth for geography.

BUILD WITH PEOPLE

Pick a house. Hire a crew. Watch it rise.

At Northline Builders, choose Harbour Courtyard 01, sign in person, and hire Jo, Ren, and Akiko. The survey, foundation, frame, roof, utilities, finishes, and handover happen as visible phases on your lot.
